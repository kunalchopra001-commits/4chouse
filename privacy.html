Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what information 4C House of Advertising LLC ("we", "us") collects through this website, why we collect it, and how we look after it.

What we collect

  • Details you send us through the booking form: your name, company, WhatsApp number, email, industry, team size, and anything you write about your business.
  • Your check-up answers, only if you complete the 60-second check-up and then submit the form. The check-up runs in your browser; nothing is sent until you press "Book my diagnostic".
  • Basic technical logs kept by our hosting provider (Netlify), such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested. These are used for security and to keep the site running.

We do not use cookies, and we do not currently use analytics or advertising tracking on this website. If we add tools such as Google Analytics, Google Ads conversion tracking or the Meta Pixel, we will update this policy first and describe what they collect.

How we use it

  • To contact you about the diagnostic call you requested, by WhatsApp, phone or email.
  • To prepare for that call and to send you a proposal if you ask for one.

We do not sell your information. We do not add you to a mailing list unless you ask us to.

Who we share it with

Only with the service providers that help us run this website and respond to you: our hosting provider (Netlify), our form provider (Formspree) and our email provider. Form submissions travel encrypted (HTTPS) from your browser to Formspree, which forwards them to our inbox. They process the data on our behalf and under their own security obligations.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as we need them to work with you, and for up to 24 months after our last contact, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Email hello@4chouse.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Cookies

This website does not set cookies. Fonts and images are served from our own site, so visiting it does not share your details with font or image services. WhatsApp and Instagram links take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.
