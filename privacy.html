Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what information 4C House of Advertising ("we", "us") collects through this website, why we collect it, and how we look after it.

What we collect

  • Details you send us through the booking form: your name, company, WhatsApp number, email, industry, team size, and anything you write about your business.
  • Your check-up answers, if you complete the 60-second check-up and then submit the form.
  • Usage data from analytics and advertising tools (Google Analytics, Google Ads and the Meta Pixel), such as pages visited, the device and browser used, and which ad brought you here.

How we use it

  • To contact you about the diagnostic call you requested, by WhatsApp, phone or email.
  • To prepare for that call and to send you a proposal if you ask for one.
  • To measure how our website and ads perform, so we spend our marketing budget sensibly.

We do not sell your information. We do not add you to a mailing list unless you ask us to.

Who we share it with

Only with the service providers that help us run this website and respond to you: our form and email providers, our CRM, and the analytics and advertising platforms named above. They process the data on our behalf and under their own security obligations.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as we need them to work with you, and for up to 24 months after our last contact, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Email inquries@4chouse.com and we will respond within 30 days. You can also block cookies in your browser settings, or adjust your ad preferences in your Google and Meta accounts.

Cookies

Our analytics and advertising tools use cookies and similar technologies to recognise returning visitors and measure ad results. The website works without them.
