FAQ

Questions owners ask us

Who is this for?

Established small and medium businesses, typically 10–200 people, who are growing but feel stretched: too many manual tasks, inconsistent marketing, or leads slipping through the cracks.

Do I need to understand AI?

No. That's our job. We explain everything in plain language and only recommend tools your team can actually use.

Will AI replace my staff?

Our aim is to take repetitive work off your team's plate so they can focus on customers and quality, and so the business doesn't depend on any one person.

Can you just run our social media?

Yes. Many clients start with AI-aided social media and lead generation. We'll still do a quick diagnostic first, so the content is aimed at the right customers and leads get followed up.

How long until we see results?

Lead capture and follow-up automations typically go live in a few weeks. Larger operational changes are phased so you see value at every step.

Do you still do fabrication, events and kiosks?

Yes, through our trusted production partners, managed by us end to end. Ask us on the call.
