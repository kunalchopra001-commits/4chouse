    Questions owners ask us.

    Who is this for?

    Established small and medium businesses, usually 10 to 200 people, that are growing but stretched: too much manual work, inconsistent marketing, or leads slipping through the cracks.

    Do I need to understand AI?

    No. That's our job. We explain everything in plain language and only recommend tools your team can realistically use.

    Will AI replace my staff?

    The aim is to take repetitive work off your team so they can focus on customers and quality, and so the business doesn't depend on any one person.

    Can you just run our social media?

    Yes. Many clients start with AI social media and lead generation. We still do a short diagnostic first, so the content reaches the right customers and every lead gets followed up.

    What does it cost?

    The first diagnostic call is free. After the diagnosis you get a written quote for each recommended fix before any work starts, so there are no surprises.

    How soon will we see results?

    Lead capture and follow-up automations usually go live within a few weeks. Bigger operational changes are phased so you see value at every step.

    Do you still build activations, stands and kiosks?

    Yes, through trusted production partners, managed by us from design to handover. Ask us on the call.
