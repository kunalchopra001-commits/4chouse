# Concept: "Biji's New Hobby" (draft, nothing produced)

A trend-jack on the GTA 6 launch. A sweet Punjabi grandmother (Biji) in a village turns mysteriously reckless overnight. The family is shocked. The reveal: she has been up all night playing an open-world game on an old CRT TV with a brand-new console.

**Format:** 30–40 seconds, vertical. Filmed as if her grandson is recording on his phone: shaky, handheld, real reactions. Punjabi lines with English subtitles.

**Why it works:**
- It rides the biggest gaming moment of the year.
- The comedy is about an innocent grandma, which feels warm, not mean.
- It speaks to the large Punjabi audience in the UAE.
- Every act is "unreal on purpose" (No-Slop check 2), so the absurdity is the joke, never a mistake.

## Sound
- A dhol-and-808 Punjabi trap beat that starts small and builds. It drops at the reveal, leaving only the soft button-click glow, then the beat kicks back in under the end card.
- Real reaction lines from the family in Punjabi: "Biji?!", "Eh ki ho reha?" (What's going on?).
- One line from Biji at the end.

## Rules this concept follows
- **No game IP on screen:** no GTA, Rockstar or PlayStation logos, no game footage, no game characters, no famous catchphrases. The console and controller are generic with no logo. The game is only implied, by her "missions" and the glow.
- **The game is named only in the caption and hashtags,** as a trend reference ("Biji got the new game too 🎮 #GTA6"). If you'd rather play it fully safe, drop the name and use "the game everyone's playing".
- **Harmless acts only:** no weapons, no police, no traffic danger, no real theft. Uncle hands over the rickshaw happily. Funny, never violent.
- **Cultural respect:** we laugh with Biji, not at her or Punjabi culture. Religious symbols are never used for jokes.
- **Fictional people:** Biji and the family are AI-made characters, labelled as AI, and never presented as a real family.
