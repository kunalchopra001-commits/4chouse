# Biji's New Hobby: screenplay and shooting plan v1 (for Kunal's approval, nothing generated)

**Runtime:** about 40 s, vertical.

**Rules for every clip:**
- **Generation:** each clip is made from its storyboard still as the **start frame**. The character references are attached so the faces stay the same. Controlled actions also get an **end frame**.
- **Clip length:** clips are 4–6 s. The edit cuts them tighter, so not every second generated is used.
- **Grade:** applied in the edit. Day shots get C (documentary natural), night shots get B (rich film).
- **Cast:** nobody looks at the lens. Biji is always focused and deadpan, absorbed in her "mission". The comedy comes from the gap between her calm and everyone else's shock.
- **Camera language:** a hidden observer on a long lens, so frames are partly blocked by walls, door frames and leaves. Small handheld drift, never shaky-cam. Slow-motion on the hero moments, quick cuts on the reactions.

## Notes
- **New stills needed** (about 3 credits): the buffalo close-up (2b), the over-shoulder rooftop shot (6a) and the close-up of the thali falling (6b). The old frame 6 is dropped.
- **Dialogue:** all lines are recorded with ElevenLabs Punjabi voices (fictional characters, never Kunal's voice) and added in the edit. Most lines are spoken off-screen or side-on, which avoids lip-sync risk. Biji's line in shot 9 is spoken in near-profile.
- **Opening music, a gurdwara-style feel:** a calm devotional morning mood (soft harmonium, a drone, birds) **without** actual Gurbani or kirtan words. Sacred hymns shouldn't sit in a comedy ad, and the Sikh audience would rightly object. This gives the same peaceful Punjab-dawn feeling respectfully.
- **Clip count:** 12 generated clips (10 from stills, plus about 2 retakes).
