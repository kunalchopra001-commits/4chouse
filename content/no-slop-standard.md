# The No-Slop Standard

The 10 checks every 4C House piece has to pass before it posts, for us and for clients. Each checks one thing. Brand line: "AI content, without the slop."

Points 1, 2 and 4 come from Kunal's own list, and point 5 was the original "checked by a human". Kunal shared this list on 19 Nov; I added points 3 and 6–10, and he has not yet confirmed them.

1. **It has a job.** Every piece has one purpose (sell, teach, prove or entertain) and one message. No aimless generation because a tool can make it.
2. **Grounded in reality, or unreal on purpose.** Products, places and people behave as they would in real life. When we break reality for fun (a dog DJing, a diver eating a card), it is obviously intentional, never a mistake.
3. **The physics and details hold.** No changing text, no objects popping in or out, no floating, no extra fingers, no plastic food, no melting faces. The scale and direction are right (for example, toothpaste goes on the brush the right way).
4. **There's a human in it.** At least one human element: a voice-over, a hum, real hands, the client's own product photos, or their own words.
5. **Checked by a human, frame by frame.** Someone with design experience watches every second before it goes out, and fixes it or kills it.
6. **Real products, true claims.** Built around the client's real products, prices and offers. No fake reviews, no invented results, and AI people are never shown as real customers.
7. **Honestly labelled.** AI-made content is labelled, on the post and on the platform.
8. **Made for this brand, not a template.** The brand's own colours, voice and customers, set in Dubai and the UAE, and respectful of local culture and moments (Ramadan, National Day).
9. **Original.** No copying other brands, artists, characters or logos. Demo brands are fictional.
10. **Worth finishing.** A hook in the first second, a clear story, sound made for the piece, and an ending that tells people what to do. If it's weak, we drop it rather than post it to fill a slot.

## How we use it
- **Before every post:** run through the 10 checks. A single fail means fix it or drop it.
- **On the website:** a "No-Slop Standard" section can sit next to the packages, so clients know what they're buying.
- **In content:** each check can become a post (for example, "Check 3: why AI food looks plastic, and how we fix it").
