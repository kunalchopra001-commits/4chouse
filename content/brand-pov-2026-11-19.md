# Brand point of view: lessons from Polaroid (19 Nov 2026)

**Decided by Kunal on 19 Nov:**
- Our enemy is **AI slop**.
- Our brand line is **"AI content, without the slop."**

Source: Brand House newsletter (Eimear), 18 Nov, an interview with Polaroid's creative director, Patricia Varella. Kunal shared it.

## What the article says
1. **Ask why you should exist at all**, not "how do we sell more". Polaroid's answer: to remind people that the best of life happens beyond screens.
2. **Name an enemy.** Polaroid's enemy is over-digitalisation, not technology itself. Standing *for* something means standing *against* something.
3. **Small budgets can't afford to be dull.** "We simply don't have the budgets for playing safe." Bold work is the cheap option.
4. **Big moments rest on quiet, brave work.** Polaroid built up to this over three and a half years, for example by shooting real people on film and turning boring how-to videos into a puppet show.
5. **Do less, and do it slower.** Optimising for efficiency kills creativity.
6. **Proof of process.** Show the making, not just the finished thing.

## What 4C House takes from it
- **Our reason to exist:** small businesses deserve content that looks like a big brand made it, without big-brand budgets or AI slop.
- **Our enemy: AI slop**, the lazy, plastic, obviously fake content now flooding feeds. Not AI itself; we use it every day. Bad AI shows when nobody checks the frames.
  - This turns our realism obsession into the brand story: the skydive v1–v6 fixes, the plastic groceries remade, the car chase swapped for a driver's view.
  - It also sets us apart from every account we benchmark. None of them names an enemy.
- **Line to test:** "AI content, without the slop." Or: "Made with AI. Checked by a human. Every frame."
- **Be bold on a small budget:** one cheeky, self-aware post a week, not more safe product loops.
- **Proof of process:** a weekly "v1 vs final" post, already planned. It is our version of Polaroid's quiet building.
- **Do less, better:** keep the realism check as a gate; a weak post gets dropped, not posted to fill a slot. This tempers the 3× frequency.

## Post ideas for week 2: "What AI can't do"
Inspired by the honesty of Polaroid's "AI can't generate sand between your toes", but the reverse: an AI studio being honest about AI. Use our own look (brand fonts and colours), not Polaroid's handwritten style.
1. Burger reel. Text: **"AI can't taste this burger. It can make you hungry."**
2. Skydive reel. Text: **"Nobody jumped out of a plane for this. Six versions to make it look like someone did."**
3. Car ad. Text: **"No camera car. No permits. One human who wouldn't post the fake version."**
4. A carousel, **"5 signs of AI slop (and how we catch them)"**: text changing between frames, objects popping in, floating physics, plastic food, hands. It is a teaching carousel, as the benchmark suggested.
5. A LinkedIn post, **"My enemy is AI slop"**: Kunal's point of view in his own words.

## Things to watch
- **Polaroid is anti-over-digitalisation, and we are an AI studio.** Borrow the method (a named enemy, bold honest lines), never their look, words or brand.
- **Every "AI can't…" line must be true.** Keep labelling everything as AI-made.
