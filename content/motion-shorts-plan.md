# Motion shorts: 8–15 s animated reels for Instagram and TikTok (plan, nothing built)

**Why:** these are cheap, quick and on-brand, and they leave nothing that can look like AI slop. There are no AI people or AI footage, only designed type and graphics. They fill the "bold-message loops", "practical tips" and "build in public" share of the pipeline (`pipeline.md`).

## Toolkit (motion-video skill check)
| Toolkit | Use? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **GSAP** | Use (default) | HTML frames animated with GSAP, rendered with Playwright and ffmpeg. Exact text, brand colours and fonts. |
| **Remotion** | Use later | Once a format works, turn it into a template for batches (e.g. one villain per week). Licence: free while 4C House has 3 people or fewer. |
| **lemo-opuscar** | Use for part | Only for an illustrated explainer style, if wanted. |
| **Three.js** | Not needed | Not needed for flat graphics. |

**Cost:** close to zero. No video credits. About 2 Higgsfield credits for each 10–15 s music track.

## Format rules
- 540×960, 24 fps, sound on, with an instrumental track (no hums or human voices).
- **Hook on screen in the first second.** No logo intro.
- One idea per reel. Text big enough to read without pausing, in the safe area.
- Where possible, the last frame flows back into the first (loops boost rewatch).
- A 2 s end card (network background, 4C tile, "Your content engine.", 4chouse.com), plus "Made by 4C House".
- No numbers or claims we can't back up.

## Series ideas
1. **Villain of the week** (10 s). A villain name slams in (The Ghost Lead, The Empty Feed, The Shoot Bill, The Vanishing Freelancer…), then its one-line pain, then the one-line 4C fix. Pairs with the week 2 talking-head skits.
2. **Spot the slop** (12 s). "5 signs an ad is AI slop", with an animated checklist ticking off: melting hands, plastic skin, floating hair, gibberish text, no purpose. Ends with "AI content, without the slop." It teaches, and it positions us.
3. **Shoot day vs content engine** (10 s). A split screen. On the left a shoot day: book a studio, hire a crew, wait for edits. On the right, 4C: brief on Monday, posts all week. Qualitative only, no prices.
4. **Before → after fix** (8 s). Build in public: our own v1 next to v2 with a wipe (e.g. the salon hair flip → natural sway; the croissant cheese ooze → real layers). Caption: "We caught this. That's the job."
5. **One-liners** (6–8 s loops). Bold type on the network background:
   - "Your competitors post daily. You post when you remember."
   - "No shoot day. Every week."
   - "Your nephew has exams."
6. **WhatsApp at 11:47pm** (12 s). An animated chat where an enquiry arrives late at night. *Only once the WhatsApp assistant is live*, so the demo is real.

## Suggested start
- Build 1 sample each of formats 1, 2 and 5 for Kunal's approval.
- Then make a week's set (3–4 reels) and template the winners in Remotion.
