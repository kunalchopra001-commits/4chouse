# Week 2: talking heads and people videos (draft for Kunal)

Kunal wants more UGC-style videos of people talking to UAE business owners, with pain points and "business villains" as the hooks.

**What already exists:**
- Four hook modes in `pipeline.md`: tension, counterintuitive claim, the owner's exact feeling, open loop.
- The rule to name an enemy (`brand-pov-2026-11-19.md`). Our brand enemy is AI slop.
- This plan adds a cast of everyday villains that UAE small-business owners fight.

## The villain cast (pain points as characters)
| Villain | The pain it stands for | Hook line |
|---|---|---|
| **The Ghost Lead** | An enquiry arrives at 11pm and nobody replies until morning | "Someone tried to pay you last night. You were asleep." |
| **The Empty Feed** | The last post was three months ago | "Your Instagram says you closed in August." |

| **The 2am Admin** | The owner does everything, including posting | "Owner, chef, cashier, social media manager. All you." |
| **The Mall Giant** | The big brand next door with a bigger budget | "They have an agency. You have a phone." |
| **The Nephew** | "My nephew does our Instagram." It's affectionate | "Your nephew is great. He also has exams." |
| **AI Slop** (our brand enemy) | Cheap, fake-looking AI content | "Fake food. Melting hands. Your customers notice." |

Each villain becomes a short series: introduce the villain in the hook, show the pain, then show how 4C House beats it.

## Formats
1. **"Villain of the week" skit (15–25 s).** An AI actor plays the villain in person, for example a smug guy in a hoodie who "eats" your leads (The Ghost Lead). Comedy, then the fix. It's clearly fiction, so it's honest and shareable.
2. **"POV: you run a … in Dubai" (15–20 s).** A café owner, salon owner or trader, played by an AI character, talks straight to camera about a pain point, UGC style: a phone held at arm's length, a real shop setting, casual speech. Each one ends with "Sound familiar?" and our line.
3. **Real Kunal, selfie video (30 s, once a week).** Kunal records on his phone and we edit it with captions and B-roll. This is the most trusted format; people buy from people.
4. **AI Kunal explains (45–60 s).** Uses the avatar script already drafted ("Why small businesses should give AI content a chance"), labelled as AI.

## Honesty rules for people videos (non-negotiable)
- **AI people are characters, never customers.** They don't say "4C House got me 30 more bookings" or anything like a testimonial. They voice a common pain, then 4C speaks as itself.
- **Labelling:** every AI person carries an "AI character" label on screen, and the platform's AI label is switched on.
- **No real business names,** real people or lookalikes.
- **Respect:** the comedy is about the problem, never about the owner. We laugh with owners, not at them.

## Draft scripts (examples)
**A. Villain: The Ghost Lead (skit, about 20 s)**
- 11.47pm, a dark café after closing. A phone lights up on the counter: "Hi, do you do catering for 50?"
- A man in a black hoodie (The Ghost Lead) leans into frame, grins at the camera and slides the phone into his pocket.
- On-screen text: "The Ghost Lead. Eats 1 in 3 late-night enquiries."
- Cut to morning. The owner reads the message: "Sorry, just saw this!" The reply ticks to read with no answer. The customer has gone elsewhere.
- Voice-over (Kunal's voice): "Your customers don't wait till morning. Our WhatsApp assistant doesn't sleep."
- End card: "AI content, without the slop." with 4chouse.com.
- *Note:* "1 in 3" must be replaced by a real, sourced figure, or dropped.

**B. POV: café owner, Al Quoz (UGC, about 15 s)**
"Okay, honest question. When did I become the photographer, the copywriter AND the social media manager? I make coffee. That's the job. *(laughs)* If you know, you know." The end card shows: "We make the content. You make the coffee. 4C House." The video is labelled "AI character".

**C. Villain: The Shoot Bill (skit, about 20 s)**
- A crisp invoice slides across a table: "Photoshoot – 1 day – AED 5,000."
- It's smug, and it grows bigger every time the owner blinks.
- Then a calendar flips, with one week of posts followed by empty days.
- Voice-over: "One shoot, one week of content. Or a whole month, every month, for less."
- *Note:* the price must be a realistic Dubai figure that we can stand behind.

**D. Real Kunal (selfie)**
"The three villains I see in every small business in Dubai." He names three of them and the fix in one line each, then ends with "Which one's yours? Comment the number."

- **Talking shots:** seedance_2_0_mini at 480p with native audio, started from a still. That's about 2.5 credits per 5 s, so about 10–15 credits per 20 s piece.
- **Voices:** ElevenLabs. Kunal's clone for voice-overs. Separate fictional voices for the characters, never Kunal's.
- **Look:** realistic phone-camera UGC for the POV pieces. Cinematic and comic for the villain skits.
- **Total for 4 AI people videos:** about 60 credits, plus stills.
