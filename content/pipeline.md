# Content pipeline

Agreed with Kunal on 15 November 2026.

## How it runs
1. Claude plans and makes each week's posts and puts them in Google Drive: **4C House – Content Pipeline** (folder id `13f2FAtyJHiQLY2AHBGeghoCNYu3Zre__`), one subfolder per week, with a "00 – Week N plan and captions" Google Doc.
2. Kunal deletes what he doesn't want, or notes changes in the plan doc.
3. Claude posts what is left at the scheduled time. Instagram (@4c.house) through Composio; LinkedIn (Kunal's personal profile) through Composio; TikTok through Higgsfield's TikTok connector, where Kunal confirms each post in the review form.
4. Every Monday: performance audit (Instagram insights; TikTok and LinkedIn as far as the connectors allow, otherwise Kunal's screenshots), added to the next week's plan.
5. After 10–12 posts on each platform (about the end of week 2), start the email lead-generation campaign.

## Frequency (3× the original plan)
- Instagram feed: about 12 a week (12.30pm and 7.30pm Dubai time).
- Instagram Stories: 10–15 a week.
- TikTok: about 12 a week (same videos as the Reels, own captions).
- LinkedIn: 5 a week, one each weekday at 8.30am. More would cut reach.

## Content mix
Showcases 35% · before → after 15% · practical tips 15% · bold-message loops 10% · build in public 15% · offer and CTA 7% · Kunal's UGC-style explainer 3% (about one every 2–3 weeks).

## Rules for every post
- AI label on (platform setting) and "AI-generated concept · 4C House" on screen; caption sign-off: "Made with AI by 4C House. Want visuals like this for your business? 4chouse.com".
- Fictional brands only; no real logos; AI people never presented as real customers.
- Instagram needs JPEG (not PNG) and a public media URL. Drive links don't work for posting: use the Higgsfield-hosted file.
- Upload limit into Drive from a session: about 3 MB per file. Bigger videos need a smaller review copy or a direct link.
